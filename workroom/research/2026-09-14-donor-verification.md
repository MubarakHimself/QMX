# QMX donor verification (2026-09-14)

Bounded primary-source check of the named donor products. No installs or authenticated testing were performed. “Advertised” means a product page claim; “documented” means a vendor README/manual explains the mechanism; “tested” is reserved for direct execution (none here). These sources are research inputs only and do not authorize adopting features.

| Donor | Primary source and verified mechanism | Status / QMX relevance |
|---|---|---|
| StrategyQuant templates | [Strategy templates docs](https://strategyquant.com/doc/strategyquant/strategy-templates/) describe templates with random placeholders that the builder fills; [What to build](https://strategyquant.com/doc/strategyquant/what-to-build/) documents custom templates, multi-symbol/timeframe generation, and improving selected strategy parts. [How it works](https://strategyquant.com/doc/strategyquant/how-does-strategyquant-work/) documents random generation and genetic evolution. | Documented. Useful donor for structured strategy hypotheses and controlled search spaces. It does not establish QMX’s schema, validation, or UI. |
| QuantAnalyzer | [Official product page](https://strategyquant.com/quantanalyzer/) advertises detailed reports, What-If, Monte Carlo, money-management simulation, portfolio construction and plugin/snippet extension. The [programming FAQ](https://strategyquant.com/doc/programming-for-sq/) repeats these capabilities and distinguishes analysis of existing results from strategy creation. | Advertised and documented; not tested. Useful donor mechanisms for result analysis, robustness, scenario comparison and portfolio exploration. Claims of “optimal” portfolios remain vendor claims requiring QMX evaluation. |
| QuantDataManager | [Official support description](https://strategyquant.com/support) documents historical-data download/import, quality checks, tables/charts, timeframe/timezone changes, conversion and export. It states integration into StrategyQuant’s Data Manager. [Download page](https://strategyquant.com/quantdatamanager/download/) confirms the product and current build/licensing context. | Documented; not tested. Strong donor for data provenance/quality/transform surfaces. Download speed and verified-download claims are vendor claims, not QMX evidence. |
| QuantConnect projects/research | [Projects](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/cloud-platform/projects) states projects contain files for backtests, research notebooks, optimizations and deployment. [Structure](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/cloud-platform/projects/structure) documents reusable libraries and parameters. [Research Pipeline](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/cloud-platform/research-pipeline) describes Ideas→Research→Backtest→Paper Trading→Live Trading cards and configurable agents. [Research deployment](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/cloud-platform/research/deployment) documents notebook nodes, sharing limits, historical data and look-ahead bias. | Documented; no account/session test. Donor for project/workspace persistence, staged evidence and research tooling. Pipeline stages and agents are product-specific and should not be treated as QMX’s required lifecycle. |
| roboquant.dev (RoboQuant) | [RoboQuant.dev](https://www.roboquant.dev/) advertises an AI trading IDE for describing strategies, code generation and debugging. [New platform page](https://www.roboquant.dev/next) describes an end-to-end idea→code→backtest→live product, closed-beta status, broker/data claims and a ground-up engine. The separate [neurallayer/roboquant repository](https://github.com/neurallayer/roboquant) is a Kotlin open-source algorithmic-trading library and links to roboquant.org. | Two distinct identities verified: roboquant.dev is a newer commercial/closed-beta AI product; roboquant.org/GitHub is the Kotlin library. Neither was tested. Avoid attributing the JVM library’s features to roboquant.dev or vice versa. |
| OpenResearch / alphaXiv | [Official site](https://openresearch.sh/) and [alphaXiv/OpenResearch README](https://github.com/alphaXiv/OpenResearch) document local-first research workspaces, parallel agents in isolated worktrees, immutable run archives tied to commits, evidence/artifact logs, local/remote compute and autoresearch loops. | Documented; not installed or run. Strong donor for reproducible experiment lineage, bounded parallel work and laptop/remote separation. Git/worktree assumptions may not fit QMX directly. |
| OpenScience | [Official repository README](https://github.com/synthetic-sciences/openscience) describes a local-first research workbench that reads literature, writes/runs code, runs experiments and records each step; it documents shell/Python/R tools, notebooks, explicit file grants, delegation and reviewable artifacts. | Documented; not tested. Donor for visible agent work, reproducibility and human judgment boundaries. Scientific-domain scope and implementation details require separate audit. |
| Delphi | [Official repository README](https://github.com/synthetic-sciences/delphi) describes a self-hosted MCP context server indexing code repositories, research papers and HuggingFace datasets, with semantic/hybrid retrieval, symbol call graphs and research sessions. It documents Docker/PostgreSQL requirements and local or hosted embeddings. | Documented; not installed. Donor for indexed evidence/context and agent-facing retrieval. Its benchmark and performance claims are vendor/repository claims, not QMX validation. |

## Verification limits

The pages confirm mechanisms and product identity, not interoperability with QMX, implementation completeness, economic value, or safe live-trading readiness. Follow-up Grok prompts should ask for source-linked capability extraction, explicit assumptions, and acceptance tests rather than copying layouts or lifecycle labels.
