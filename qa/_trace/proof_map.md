# QMX verification phase — proof map

**Scope.** Consolidation of 23 per-epic L6 requirements-fidelity adjudications (`qa/epics/*/L6-REVIEW.md`),
their lane `findings.csv` / `RESULTS.md`, and the machine battery (tier-1 scanners, Skylos, vulture,
3-way dead-code intersection, mutmut, coverage) against `integration@2c8d495`.

**Authority order.** L6 adjudications are authoritative on test-derived findings; the raw lane
`findings.csv` files contain rows L6 struck as wrong-expectation or mis-scoped (E2-F01/E2-F02 re-filed
to Epic 12, E4-F01 factually disproved, E12-F01 `observed` column re-adjudicated, E17-F04 re-classified
from UNPROVEN to a defect). Those corrections are carried into `qa/_trace/findings.csv`.

**Reconciliation note.** `_bmad-output/test-artifacts/` is absent from the verification worktree and
16 of 23 lanes independently confirmed it. Every lane therefore reconstructed the L0–L6 taxonomy, the
15 P0/P1 assertions and its own risk-gate ids from its task brief. **This document is the first
reconciliation against the real handoff** (`_bmad-output/test-artifacts/test-design/QMX-handoff.md`),
so several per-lane P0/P1 labels move here.

**Totals.** 1,379 tests written across 23 lanes. 131 consolidated findings: 44 CONFIRMED, 64 UNPROVEN,
23 VERIFICATION-DEBT. By severity: 4 critical, 48 high, 61 medium, 18 low.

## Part 3 — how to read the two numbers that look contradictory

Five lanes returned **zero findings** (E3, E10, E13, E14 with empty files; E20 with three non-defect
rows). Four of those five are T1 or T2 epics. In every case the L6 seat found the same thing: the
zero is an accurate report of what was *executed* and an overstatement of what was *verified*, because
the assertion carrying the gate could not fail — an orphaned guard checked against its own lookup
table (E10), a dict re-hash standing in for a run (E14), a constant compared to itself (E13), a
label-quantifier standing in for a path-quantifier (E3).

Conversely, the two lanes that filed the most reds — E6 (15 failures, none softened) and E18 (5 rows,
3 distinct defects) — are among the more trustworthy in the set. **Red count is not a quality signal
here; falsifiability is.** The fix cards are ordered on confirmed damage, and the verification-debt
rows exist so the operator can see which greens are worth what.
