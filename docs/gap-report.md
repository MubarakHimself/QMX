## Ratification handoff

To close an open gap, the operator must state the ruling, the ledger must record its standing and provenance, affected contracts and component specs must be updated, and validation must pass. Editing a recommendation into imperative prose is not ratification. The 44 answered gaps have crossed the first step — an operator ruling exists in the ledger — but the corpus stays `provisional` until the whole knowledge base is re-ratified; no answered gap authorizes implementation or live money on its own. This report is the docs-local navigation surface, not a signature record.
