## 2. Final QMF V1 scope

### 2.1 Load-bearing definition

QMF is the toolbox used to build applications; it is not the trading node, the backtesting runtime, or the QMX app. Application flow such as bot → Book → BMS → broker must remain outside the framework foundation. This was the decisive correction after the earlier “kernel” diagram accidentally redrew the trading node. [SRC-02-C0029] [SRC-02-C0030] [SRC-02-C0031]

The later operating principle is **open toolbox, shared contracts**: QMF must remain usable beside ordinary Python and external libraries or methods. Uniformity comes from stable contracts and conventions, not a closed world. Stronger restriction belongs in the future agentic harness and at the human-controlled promotion boundary into live money. [SRC-01-C0003] [SRC-01-C0004] [SRC-01-C0013]

The phrase **“minimal core” is retired for the overall agreement**. The agreement is the **QMF V1 blueprint**; `qmf-core` is merely its small first brick. [SRC-01-C0026] [SRC-01-C0027]

## 4. Unresolved conflicts and open gaps

### Stage 4 blockers for honest documentation

1. **The six “frozen” choices are not all ratified.** Earlier proposals included UTC-nanosecond clock details, instrument identity, canonical indicator arithmetic, backtest-fidelity labels, a strategy-quality threshold, and result-key labeling. QMX2 repeatedly records them as pending explicit confirmation in the documentation pass. No doc should quietly freeze them. [SRC-01-C0010] [SRC-01-C0013] [SRC-01-C0016] [SRC-01-C0025] [SRC-01-C0029]
2. **Roster inclusion is stronger than internal-contract approval.** `qmf-registry` and `qmf-data` are in the final roster, but their detailed schemas came from studies. The operator praised/accepted the direction, while the assistant itself labeled the studies “delivered” and pending ratification. [SRC-01-C0020] [SRC-01-C0021] [SRC-01-C0022] [SRC-01-C0029]
3. **Bot cardinality conflict:** the older/final draft says a bot is one confluence plus a Book binding; the later direct correction allows a bot to contain multiple confluences. Use `1..n` as the likely direction, but record a GAP and obtain explicit confirmation. [SRC-02-C0030] [SRC-02-C0031] [SRC-01-C0012] [SRC-01-C0013]
4. **Exit ownership conflict:** the operator repeatedly places exits in Book territory, while the GitBook baseline gives bots exit organs and Books forced exits; dynamic SL/TP moved toward Book grammar later. This belongs to the risk/node session and must not be settled in `qmf-core`. [SRC-02-C0030] [SRC-02-C0031] [SRC-01-C0010] [SRC-01-C0011]
5. **Acquisition boundary:** QMF V1 includes `qmf-data` ingest plumbing, but an earlier correction places downloading/scheduling as a QMX app feature. The likely seam is “QMF supplies source contracts/adapters; the app owns lifecycle and scheduling,” but that is an inference requiring ratification. [SRC-02-C0030] [SRC-02-C0031] [SRC-02-C0037] [SRC-01-C0029]
6. **Dependency policy needs one crisp law.** The operator rejects code transplantation and foreign domain contracts but later insists QMF work with external Python libraries. Specify the difference between a permitted dependency/wrapper and prohibited transplantation; name the licence allowlist rather than inheriting an assistant proposal.
