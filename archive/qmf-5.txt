  fine; the engine takeover is the blocker. It's also gone stale — the version
  you'd actually install is from June 2024, and the newer one was pulled off the
  package index.

  2. The clean path exists and is better anyway. Spotware publishes the raw
  protocol definitions — four files that ARE the API's vocabulary, MIT-licensed,
  versioned by numbered releases (currently release 91). We compile those
  ourselves and write our own thin connection layer. That fits the build-our-own
  ruling you made back on day one, and it means every protocol change Spotware
  ships arrives as a numbered, diffable release we can track — instead of hoping
  a stale SDK catches up.

  nothing responsible with credentials — secrets pasted straight into the code,

    handful of daily bars and measures where the broker actually rolls, storing
    it as per-broker fact. Our own trading-day calendar stays our choice

  GAP-0036 → Order safety (built on YOUR node rules — nothing invented)

  Five laws, all traced to your corpus: (1) exactly four commands — place,
  cancel, close-position, close-all; amending is absent until deliberately
