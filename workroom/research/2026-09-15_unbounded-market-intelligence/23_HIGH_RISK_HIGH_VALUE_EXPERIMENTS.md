# 23 — High-risk, high-value experiments

These can fail and still teach. They are **not** V1 binds.

1. **Wrong-target test (high value).** Train LightGBM on forward-range quantiles, evaluate on 20996 compression labels (and vice versa). If each model only wins on its own label, the “regime” slot is an objective in disguise, not a market object.
2. **Transfer.** Freeze FX-major artifact, score gold and a crypto-spot series **without refit**. Failure is the default; a win would be surprising and would challenge “fx-majors-declared-roster.”
3. **Changeover-only Book.** Ignore classes; only CUSUM/BOCPD cooldown. If this captures most of the door value, GAP-0051’s classifier is overkill.
4. **Flicker vs dwell.** Jump-penalty / hysteresis / min-dwell on any discrete class. If dwell is doing the work, the model family is secondary.
5. **Cost-on, latency-on.** Re-score E1 with spread-as-cost and a one-bar delay. Many article edges die here (9804/22772 lesson).

Penalties: look-ahead, future smoothing, binary float identities, online learning, hidden live authority, metaphor-only, selection across many article ideas without a holdout.
