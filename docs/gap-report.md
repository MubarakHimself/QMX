## Ratification handoff

To close a gap, the operator must state the ruling, the ledger must record its standing and provenance, affected contracts and component specs must be updated, and validation must pass. Editing a recommendation into imperative prose is not ratification. The review prompts and rationale are preserved in `_docwork/ratification-packet.md`; this report is the docs-local navigation surface, not a signature record.
