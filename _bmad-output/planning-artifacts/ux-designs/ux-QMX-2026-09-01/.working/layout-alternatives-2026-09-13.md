## Department Rooms

Global slim launcher; horizontal durable world tabs; active Research world owns a local sidebar and purposeful overview. Inquiries are opened from the overview; tool entry points are local and need not form a sequence. A selected inquiry links its materials and next useful action.

Question tested: should entering a world orient the person before they descend into the work?

Trade-off: clear domain ownership and discoverability, but another navigation step between switching a world and resuming a specific task. A real implementation should restore current work when appropriate rather than always force the overview.

## Paired Worlds

Global launcher and horizontal durable tabs; optional pairing opens two complete worlds together, each retaining its own header and local tools. The example is Library plus Trading Node inspecting the same bot version, with no operations performed. A focus control returns either world to full width.

Question tested: should cross-world work preserve both full environments rather than navigating away or adding a generic details dock?

Trade-off: context remains visible, but two worlds compete for laptop width. Pairing is optional, not a required default. Saved layouts, minimum widths, resizing and keyboard behavior need specification after selection.
