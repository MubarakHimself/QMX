## 1. The Book (GitBook baseline)

**Definition (verbatim):** *"A pod with charter, capital, roster, profile, rules, and journals. A book controls bots and never trades directly."* (`05-trading-node-primer.md:41`). A Book *"owns the money and the permission"* — not a strategy, not a container (`:52`). Never-list: may never *"trade directly, revive the dead uniform-values-across-books idea, revive the dead session-windows-as-authority idea, or redistribute unclaimed budget"* (`:56`).

**Template/instance split (ADR-0002):** template written once as *"sealed Sections 0–5"* carrying grammar; each instance owns its own values in the registry under the owning instance (`:60-64`). Law **L2**: *"Infrastructure after book-template Section 5 is global multi-book capability; a book profile selects capability and leaves unused capability dormant"* (`:65`). **Dormant socket** = *"A fully specified global capability switched off by a book profile. The capability is not deleted"* (`:66`). Section count: seven indices, six sealed, **Section 6 is `GAP-0001`** — FM-4: *"Mark GAP(GAP-0001) and do not invent workspace behavior"* (`:68`). Section ordinals (from K-30 delta, matches baseline ordering but the numbering-to-name mapping is not printed in GitBook): *"Sections 0–5 mean charter, footprint, money rules, entrance exam, leash chain, capacity/sweep. There is no current Section 6."* (`:74`; `trading-node-delta.md:83`).

### The seven doors (fixed order)
*"footprint, viability veto, R_max, daily budget, breaker, exposure ledger, and kill switch"* (`05-trading-node-primer.md:78`). Chain: intent(CT-BOOK-01) → footprint → viability → rmax → budget → breaker → exposure → KSA → adapter (`:82-94`). Door meanings: footprint (measured envelope, carries `footprint_version`); viability veto (`FORM-0007`: `round_trip_cost_R/expected_edge_R <= v_cost`, `v_cost=0.10`); R_max (`FORM-0006`: `R_max_usd <= B*b*Lbar`); daily budget (`FORM-0003`: `D=U/n`); breaker (bench to paper after `scalper_breaker_threshold` consecutive stop-outs, auto-reset next open, DEC-0032); exposure ledger (BMS desk, cross-book cap = `GAP-0008`); kill switch (KSA) (`:98-106`). Two universal rules: **every refusal is written** (L11; FM-2 *"Append refusal and reason to veto ledger"*; CT-BOOK-01 *"Every refusal emits CT-BMS-05"*) (`:110`); **doors not the clock own permission** — DEC-0025 killed session-windows-as-authority: *"the clock alone does not authorize trades… Doors and KSA own permission"* (`:111`).

### The money ladder (registry+formula owned)
Cap `FORM-0001 C = cap_multiple*S`; Runway `FORM-0002 U = E-K`; Daily budget `FORM-0003 D = U/n`; Offer per seat `FORM-0004 offer_R_usd = D/(B*b*Lbar)`; Take per seat `FORM-0005 take_R_usd = min(offer_R_usd, trust_bounded_cost_aware_kelly_R_usd)` (`:117-123`). **Scalper instance values** (`work/gitbook-baseline.md:118-128`): `S=500 USD`, `K=200 USD` (fixed within cycle), `cap_multiple=2.5`, `n=5`, `B=2` (consecutive stop-outs), `b=2`, `Lbar=measured per bot at exam` (0.35R is reference only, never inherited), `v_cost=0.10`, `max_concurrent_live_bots=3`. Three warnings: `Lbar` is measured never assumed (`05-trading-node-primer.md:127`); Kelly half is deliberately unfinished (`:128`); worked numbers are **checksums to recompute, never to copy** (`:129`, `:31`). Constitutional money rule **L4/DEC-0021**: *"Unclaimed or freed budget is never redistributed in-cycle"* (`:131`).

### Cycles, sweep, treasury boundary
*"The cycle is seed to cap. The book compounds within a cycle and ratchets between cycles"* (DEC-0006) (`:137`); *"Sweep is checked at rollover only. If cap is hit intraday, the book completes the day and sweep uses rollover equity"* (DEC-0038) (`:138`). **Only three events cross the book-to-treasury boundary**: `sweep`, `refund`, `re_seed` (CT-BMS-01) (`:152`). Dead paths: mid-cycle top-up (DEC-0020) and live restart from kill-line remnant (DEC-0023, *"Kill line means paper mode until re-seed at a cycle boundary"*) (`:156`).

### Exits and stop authority (read narrowly)
Baseline: *"The bot owns market-facing entry and exit organs. The book owns admission, sizing, doors, leash, and profile selection"* (`:162`). The Book does **not** own the ordinary exit; it owns **forced** exit via leash + KSA effects at the Adapter (`:164`). Named forced-exit powers: hold-time force-flat (leash last rung, named once never defined), kill-line stand-down (`GAP-0006`), classed kill switch (KSA effect), day closure (leash rung, named once never defined) (`:167-173`). **[later delta]** K-35: *"Dynamic SL/TP belongs to Book money-rule grammar, with BMS configuration authority and Adapter enforcement. A globally uniform stop service is rejected."* and K-36/PE-7 leaves position fate at rollover/sweep/kill/paper boundaries **open** (`:174`).

### The leash (seven rungs)
*"ambient governor, day closure, bench-to-paper, chorus flag, kill-line stand-down, classed kill switch, and hold-time force-flat"* (DEC-0037) (`:178`). **L12**: *"Graduated policy shrinks before it blocks unless the event class demands instant action"* (`:182`). **L16**: *"The leash handles damage; sunset review handles pointlessness"* (`:183`). Only 3 of 7 rungs are defined: bench-to-paper (DEC-0032, the only ratified paper transition), chorus flag (DEC-0048, *"owns rate and clustering shape, not amount lost"*; frequency rule `null`/`GAP-0012`), kill-line stand-down (`GAP-0006`) (`:186-189`). Ambient governor, day closure, classed-kill-switch target levels, hold-time force-flat: **named but never defined** (`:191`). Human review escape hatch closed: DEC-0022 killed the human chorus review loop — *"intraday review latency is incompatible with market action"* (`:193`). Legacy ancestry map for each rung at `recovery-lineage-addendum.md:135-143` (donor behavior only; PE-5 mapping needs ratification).

### Book modes
CT-BOOK-02 and CT-BMS-02 share one enum: **`LIVE, PAPER, BENCHED, STOOD_DOWN`** (`05-trading-node-primer.md:197`). Every mode record carries `reason` + `trigger_decision` matching `DEC-[0-9]{4}` — *"a mode never changes without naming the decision that changed it"* (`:197`). Contract rules: *"Paper balances freeze at mode flip"*; breaker bench-to-paper auto-resets next open (DEC-0032); *"Other paper/live promotion, freeze, demotion, and return semantics remain GAP-0006"* (`:197`). **[later delta]** K-26/C-02 say the four values mix two namespaces (Book mode vs roster-seat state) and must be split (`:199`; `trading-node-delta.md:72,154`).

## 2. The BMS (four desks)

*"BMS accounts for and constrains books. It has Treasury, Exposure, Records, and Reporting desks, and it never trades, sizes, or reaches inside a book"* (DEC-0045) (`05-trading-node-primer.md:227`). May: *"own virtual ledger state, exposure measurement, mode registry, append-only journals, reporting metrics, KSA policy, and news block directives"* (`:231`). May never: *"trade directly, mutate bot logic, overwrite journals in place, or bypass the veto ledger"* (`:231`).

- **Treasury desk** — owns the virtual capital ledger and book-to-treasury boundary; only sweep/refund/re-seed cross (DEC-0038); refund reserve `FORM-0008 reserve_usd ~= rho*N_cycles_month*S` with `rho` and `N_cycles_month` both `null`/`GAP-0007` (`:252`).
- **Exposure desk** — measures exposure, emits news block directive `CT-BMS-04` to KSA; SCN-0003 proves a news directive refuses entries for **live and paper alike** (L9, DEC-0010); v2 authority incl. cross-book cap = `GAP-0008` (`:254`).
- **Records desk** — the spine: *"Records is append-only and owns the only journal write path"* (DEC-0046); correction rule FM-1: *"Append a correction entry referencing the corrected entry"* (`:256`). **Five journals**: veto ledger, KSA audit log, trade journal, book journal, correlation ledger (`:258-264`). FM-3: *"A no is not journaled → Treat as violation of DEC-0012"* (`:266`).
- **Reporting desk** — *"Reporting computes from Records and has zero authority"* (`:268`).

**Reconciliation + technical kill:** `CT-BMS-03` carries `virtual_equity`, `broker_equity`, `explained_delta`, `verdict ∈ {reconciled|drift|unknown}`, rule *"Unexplained drift is a technical kill"*; `reconciliation_epsilon = 0` with `operator_review: true` (*"operator review is mandatory before non-zero use"*); incident playbook: *"If CT-BMS-03 returns drift, halt trading as a technical kill"* (`:294`). Authority model in one line: **The bot proposes; the Book admits and sizes; the BMS accounts and constrains** (`:288`). ADR-0001 design test: *"New components must declare whether they sense, decide, account, or execute"* (`:290`). **[later delta]** K-46B/C-23: CT-BMS-03 and CT-BMS-01 directions need repair (Treasury→BMS); C-07 suspects a stale `GAP-0010` cross-ref where `GAP-0008` was meant (`:296`).

## 3. The other organs

- **MIS / SQS** — MIS split into MIS-Live (hot snapshots) + MIS-Archive (immutable emissions for replay); information-only (L6: *"MIS never sizes, blocks, or trades"*); computes each labeler/version/param/pair/resolution **once** and fans out (DEC-0041); failure is conservative (degraded field + `degraded_sensors`; `sqs_hard_block` forces door refusal; dead `feed_state` blocks new entries); L10 binds exam↔live labeler versions (`05-trading-node-primer.md:305`). GitBook **never expands SQS**; only `sqs_score`/`sqs_hard_block` fields + *"SQS unreachable → Door performs hard block"* (`:306`). **[operator ruling, K-38]** SQS = **Spread Quality Sensor**: compares instrument-aware historical spread with current live spread, emits score/hard-block evidence, grants MIS no trade authority; `sqs_score = historical_average_spread / current_live_spread` (1.0 = baseline, >1.0 tighter, <1.0 wider) — a strong recovery candidate; thresholds/hysteresis/cadence/windows need ratification (`trading-node-delta.md:96`; `recovery-lineage-addendum.md:75-92`). **D-09 (DROP):** any expansion of SQS as snapshot quality score / signal quality score / Simple Queue Service, or SQS as execution authority — *"MIS transports; the Book door decides"* (`trading-node-delta.md:190`).
- **KSA** — *"the global protection state machine. BMS owns policy, the trading node enforces effects through the adapter, and bots never see KSA directly"* (DEC-0008) (`05-trading-node-primer.md:308`). See §4a for the verbatim levels/classes/law.
- **Adapter** — broker-facing boundary, the **only** thing that reaches a broker; platform-blind; maintains account binding; four commands `place_order, cancel_order, close_position, close_all`; unknown startup state emits `unknown_state` and **blocks broker execution until reconciled**; cTrader feasibility = `GAP-0005` (`:312`).
- **Paper mode** — *"Paper mode is diagnostic. It freezes the counterfactual balance at flip and preserves evidence after a breaker, kill-line stand-down, or demotion"* (L13, DEC-0014); only ratified transition is the breaker path; other transitions = `GAP-0006` (`:315`).
- **Treasury cycles** — seed→cap; compound within, ratchet between; sweep checked at rollover only; re-seed at cycle boundary only (`:318`).
- **Examination** — certifies a bot against a **specific book**; gates edge-after-costs + non-fiction; may never authorize live trading (`:321`). *(Certification is Backend Node / out of node scope; the node holds only operative certificate validity — see §4e boundary.)*
- **Notification** — journal-derived, operator-facing, forbidden from asking intraday trade judgment (DEC-0004); everything about severity/channels/retry/dedupe/quiet-hours/credentials = `GAP-0002`; ENH-0001 tiers interim only (`:323`).
- **Data layer** — *"mostly a gap in this run"*; two stable islands (immutable MIS archive, append-only BMS journals); everything else = `GAP-0003`; explicit warning against selecting DBs/retention/migration/backup from the old vault without ratification (`:325`).
- **QML** — in-scope component shell; interfaces = `GAP-0013`; CT-QML-01 registers zero interfaces; may never become an agentic workflow surface in this deterministic pass (`:327`).

### (e) What the primer says QMF must provide the node vs what the node itself owns

**QMF must provide the node** (`05-trading-node-primer.md:451-479`, each row traced to a baseline page in the file):
exact money arithmetic (sweep = equity−seed, `reconciliation_epsilon=0`, drift is a kill); a registry of values + a registry of formulas with configurable coefficients (no hardcoded load-bearing numbers; compute-once fan-out snapshots with version stamps; explicit degradation, never silent defaults; latency measurement per hop against registry budgets (tick-to-MIS 35 ms, order 10–45 ms, end-to-end 100 ms);
