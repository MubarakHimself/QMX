## Verdict: **gaps**

This is a strong suite. It is materially better than its siblings on the dimension that matters most for a live-money boundary: the assertions are drawn from the CT-* YAML rather than from the code, the fakes are genuine injected seams, and the two hardest behaviours — the read-time transition matrix (`L3-008`, enumerated over `OrderState × ObservationKind`, not hand-picked) and the "measured, never hardcoded" broker configuration (`L3-010`, which measures **ASK** and **UTC minute 480** precisely so a hardcoded BID/17:00-NY would fail) — are asserted the way the requirement demands. `L3-005`/`L4-001` track SCN-0005 faithfully, including the counter-intuitive turn that a *reconciled* verdict still holds a standing intent open while the block stands. All three `findings.csv` rows are genuine.

The verdict is `gaps` for three reasons, in order of consequence:

1. **The UNKNOWN block — the single load-bearing invariant of this epic — is proven on exactly one stream.** Every test in the suite constructs one venue and one account (`"ACC-1"`, the `mk_account` default) and one binding. CT-19's stream-granularity invariant, which states both bounds explicitly, is untested in both directions. RESULTS reports `L3-001..007` and `L4-001` as PASS and the block as proven.
2. **CT-18's amend-atomicity verify-or-refuse rule has zero tests and zero deferral notes.** "Until verified, dual-side protection amendment refuses and single-sided is the only legal path" is a protection rule on the money path, sitting in the exact nullability family PLAN §3 row (a) flagged as present in this epic. It appears in neither the plan's Section 4 nor RESULTS' "not testable this phase".
3. **Three assertions the PLAN declared were narrowed at implementation without being recorded** — the L1-001 export-surface sweep, L2-005's undeclared *order parameter*, and L2-022's "sequence resets only on boot with a cursor durable through the observation sink". Two of them are the parts of their plan rows that would have failed.

Nothing here disturbs the four-outcome law (`L2-007/008`), the fail-closed error map (`L2-002`), the record-before-interpret ordering (`L2-012`), the secret boundary (`L1-002`, `L2-019/020/021`), or the structural isolation checks (`L3-014`) — those are proven to the standard RESULTS claims for them.

## 1. Wrong-expectation tests

Nine items. None produces a false finding — every one of these currently passes — but each asserts something narrower than, or other than, what the requirement demands. Ranked by consequence.

### W1 — `L2-001`'s roster assertion is circular and can never fail

- **Test:** `test_l2_capabilities.py::test_l2_001_declaration_static_credential_free_tag91_identity_bearing`.
- **What it asserts:** `assert set(decl.fields) == set(CapabilityFieldName)`.
- **Why it is circular:** `_helpers.build_declaration` builds the field list with `for name in CapabilityFieldName:`. The left side is constructed *from* the right side. The assertion holds for any enum whatsoever, including one missing half the roster.
- **What the requirement actually says:** CT-18 invariant — *"CT-18 **owns the field roster**: market-data kinds, order-parameter subset, command scopes, acknowledgement modes per command kind, position model netting|hedging per account, session topology, throttle scope, rate limits, span caps and paging model, token lifecycle class, equity nativeness, server-clock availability, instrument-metadata surface, attribution-label support, and protection primitives … the risk gate added — all measured-at-connection and verify-or-refuse — account settlement currency, the margin surface, the instrument value-factor metadata surface, the reconciliation lookback declaration, and the protection-capability fields."* That is a literal list of 20 names the test should have written down and compared against.
- **No defect behind it:** the implementation's roster does carry all 20 CT-18 names. The test is a tautology guarding a currently-correct roster, not a false pass — but it cannot catch a regression, and it is the one assertion standing between CT-18's roster clause and nothing.
- **Two further claims in the test's own name are never asserted:** *credential-free* (no assertion that the declaration contains no `SecretRef`/`SecretValue`/credential material) and *static* (no assertion that it is importable without credentials or that it holds no measured or tunable value). Both are verbatim CT-18 invariant clauses.
- **Same defect, same file:** `test_l2_001_declaration_identity_excludes_measured_values` iterates `H.DEFAULT_MEASURED` — the QA helper's own set — so it asserts consistency with a QA-side choice, not with CT-18's named measured-at-connection list. Nothing anywhere asserts that `settlement_currency`, `margin_surface`, `value_factor_metadata`, `reconciliation_lookback`, and `protection_capabilities` **are** measured-at-connection and cannot be declared static; `build_declaration` never attempts `CapabilityField.static(SETTLEMENT_CURRENCY, …)`, which CT-18's nullability clause forbids.

### W2 — `L1-001` is a hand-picked sample presented as an enumeration, and the one callable found to raise was excluded rather than recorded

- **Test:** `test_l1_properties.py::test_l1_001_public_boundary_never_raises` over the module-level `CALLABLES` list.
- **What the PLAN promised (§4, L1-001):** *"**Every public callable enumerated from the `qmf.venue` export surface** returns a value or a typed refusal and never raises for any generated well-typed input."*
- **What was built:** 47 lambdas typed out by hand. `qmf.venue.__all__` exports 136 names. The sweep is not an enumeration and does not become one if the roster changes; a new public factory added tomorrow is silently outside R-002's claim.
- **The consequential half:** the `CALLABLES` comment states *"The mapping/predicate helpers (journal_event_type, is_* ...) are excluded: they are documented to take typed enums, not untrusted input."* RESULTS then records that `observation_journal_event_type(kind)` **raises `ValueError`** on a non-`ObservationKind` and classifies it as *"an observation, not a failing finding"* because L1-001 is scoped to well-typed input. The exclusion was written after the behaviour was known. R-002's claim, as PLAN §1 states it, is *"no public callable raises"* — and the symbol is in `__all__`. SCN-0005's Given is likewise unqualified: *"every public venue boundary succeeds or returns a typed refusal."* A programmatic enumeration would have produced this as a finding; a hand-list produced it as a footnote. Either the exclusion is right and the plan's universal wording is wrong, or the row belongs in `findings.csv`; the lane resolved that tension in the direction of a green sweep without flagging the choice.

### W3 — Three assertions read the implementation's testimony about itself

- **Tests:** `test_l3_ctrader.py::test_l3_016_session_recovery_never_resubmits` (`assert SessionRecovery.resubmits_command is False`, asserted a second time inside `test_l3_blocking.py::test_l3_007_…`); `test_l3_016_heartbeat_bound_span_cap_and_two_host_topology` (`assert SessionTopology.required_connection_count == 2`).
- **What the requirement says:** Story 8.8 AC-3 — *"session recovery **never resubmits a command**"*; CT-19 — *"session recovery is the adapter's declared work run by the application's scheduler and **never resubmits a command**."*
- **Why this is the wrong shape:** a class attribute declaring `resubmits_command = False` is the code asserting its own compliance. An implementation that resubmits *and* carries the flag passes. The behavioural half exists — `SessionRecovery().on_disconnect(["cmd-1","cmd-2"])` returning UNKNOWN resolutions in `L3-007` — and is the only part of this that can fail; the flag assertions add a false sense of coverage and should not be counted toward AC-3.

### W4 — `L2-018` over-specifies CT-20's cardinality clause into a naming-scheme pin

- **Test:** `test_l2_events.py::test_l2_018_journal_mapping_is_total_and_unique_over_command_outcomes`.
- **What it asserts:** `len(events) == len(CommandKind) * len(SubmissionOutcome)` — i.e. all 25 `(kind, outcome)` pairs must map to 25 *distinct* event-type strings.
- **What the requirement says:** CT-20 — *"exactly **one** journal event per recorded observation, one per submission, one per outcome."* That is a cardinality law (one, not zero, not two). It does not require the event *type strings* to be pairwise distinct across the full cross-product, and the cross-product includes pairs the four-outcome law forbids for a single command (any non-compound kind × `partially-executed`). The test pins the implementation's naming convention and would fail a conformant implementation that named, say, every denied-locally event identically.
- **The cardinality the contract does demand is only half-covered:** `L2-007` asserts `result.observation is not None and result.journal_event is not None` — field presence on the returned object, not that exactly one journal event was appended through the injected sink. Only the recorder path (`L2-012`, `len(jnl_sink.calls) == 1`) actually counts writes.

### W5 — `L2-016`'s "never gates sensing" reads a constant off a directly-constructed dataclass

- **Test:** `test_l2_events.py::test_l2_016_reconciliation_gates_command_pipe_only_never_sensing`, which does `Reconciliation(verdict=v, detail="test")` and asserts `recon.gates_sensing_pipe is False`.
- **What the requirement says:** CT-20 — *"Reconciliation gates the command pipe only — **the sensing pipe never blocks on it**"*; SCN-0005 — *"Throughout, the sensing pipe never blocks."*
- **Why it falls short:** the object is constructed directly (bypassing any factory), and `gates_sensing_pipe` is a constant property. No pipe is observed. The behavioural face — a `drift` / `unknown` / `out-of-lookback` verdict in force while a sensing observation still flows — is never exercised. `L3-002` and `L4-001` prove the sensing pipe keeps flowing under an **UNKNOWN** block; they do not cover the reconciliation case, which is the one CT-20's sentence is about.

### W6 — `L2-013` normalizes an API shape CT-19 forbids, and asserts a refusal no authority states

- **Tests:** `test_l2_events.py::test_l2_013_terminal_state_only_from_fills_and_lifecycle_never_from_absence` and `::test_l2_013_denied_locally_has_no_venue_order`.
- **The shape:** `fold_order_state(SubmissionOutcome.ACCEPTED_BY_VENUE, [...])` takes the **command outcome** as the fold's seed. CT-19's invariant is *"Command outcome and order state are **separate streams, never merged**: an order's terminal state is decided by fills and venue lifecycle events under CT-20 only, **never inferred from a command outcome**."* CT-20 states state is *"a read-time fold **over the observation stream**"*.
- **What the tests do about it:** they assert the weaker consequence (an accepted-by-venue seed with no observations is not terminal) and accept the merged signature as given. The separation itself — the clause the invariant actually states — is asserted nowhere.
- **The second test:** `fold_order_state(DENIED_LOCALLY, [])` returning a refusal is not a stated requirement anywhere in CT-19/CT-20. It is a reasonable implementation choice being pinned as if it were contract.

### W7 — Four assertions prove a name is absent rather than that a behaviour is impossible

- **Tests:** `L3-007` (`for forbidden in ("flatten","close_all","retry","assume_outcome","invent_terminal"): assert not hasattr(gate, forbidden)`); `L3-012` (`("submit","place_order","send_order","order")` on the probe); `L2-012` (`assert not hasattr(good, "state")` / `"order_state"`); `L2-020` (`("reveal","secret_value","get_secret","secrets","secret_for")` on the connection manager).
- **Judgement:** these are defensible structural proxies and `L2-020`'s is the strongest of them (it is paired with real repr/health-report checks against a real plaintext). But CT-19 states the rule in its **positive, closed** form — *"its own `adapter_self` actions are **limited to** suspend_new, drain, throttle, and session state"* — which is assertable as a set equality over the declared action surface. A blocklist of five guessed names is weaker than the closed set the contract publishes, and it silently passes if the capability arrives under a sixth name.

### W8 — `L1-002`'s ref-id assertion is half-vacuous

- **Test:** `test_l1_properties.py::test_l1_002_secret_value_never_renders_its_value`, line `assert _REF.value in render or render == str(sv)`.
- The right-hand disjunct is trivially true for four of the six render paths (`str`, `format`, f-string, `%s`, `.format`), so *"only the ref id is rendered"* is genuinely checked for `repr` alone. The load-bearing half — `secret not in render` — is asserted for all six and is correct; this is a lesser blemish on an otherwise good property.

### W9 — `L3-009` counts the money-bearing messages without naming them

- **Test:** `test_l3_ctrader.py::test_l3_009_money_decode_governs_nine_messages_absent_exponent_refuses`, line `assert len(MONEY_BEARING_MESSAGES) == 9`.
- Story 8.8 AC-1 and DEC-0135 speak of *"a `moneyDigits` exponent on **the nine** money-bearing messages"*. A roster with the right cardinality and the wrong membership passes. The rest of this test (present exponent decodes exactly; absent exponent is `unavailable dependency`, never a default to 2; a message outside the nine is refused) is exactly right.

## 4. What holds up

Recorded so the re-work is scoped and the good work is not redone.

- **`L3-008` (both functions)** — the best test in the epic. It enumerates `OrderState × ObservationKind`, asserts the biconditional `edge_produced == (not legal)` over every cell, and asserts the matrix genuinely contains forbidden transitions (`illegal_seen > 0`) so a vacuously-legal matrix cannot pass. That is precisely the shape PLAN §3 row (h) demanded and precisely what line coverage cannot see.
- **`L3-010` (all three)** — measures **ASK** and **UTC minute 480**, then asserts the minted calendar contains `utc_minute_of_day=480` and contains neither `17:00` nor `New_York`/`America`. It would fail a hardcoded BID/17:00-NY implementation, which is the entire point of AR-46/DEC-0135. `test_l3_010_config_names_no_broker_only_opaque_identity` asserts the deployment identity is *exactly* `{venue_id, account_id}` — a closed-set assertion, not a blocklist.
- **`L3-005` / `L4-001`** — the standing-intent turn is asserted the hard way round: a **reconciled** verdict still yields `HOLD_OPEN` while the block stands, and only dispatches after an explicit `resolve_unknown`. That is SCN-0005's counter-intuitive clause and the easy thing to get wrong.
- **`L2-002` (both)** — the fail-closed default is asserted on all four axes (`mapped`, outcome class, refusal category, retryability, alarm), and the same code in an *undeclared context* is shown to fail closed. Category-alone-never-implies-retryability is genuinely proven.
