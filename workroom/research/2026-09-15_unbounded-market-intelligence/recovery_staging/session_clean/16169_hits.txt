For context, we need to know when the trading session will end to close trades be
262: Markets can be highly volatile overnight, especially in response to economic events, geopolitical developments, or news from other global markets. Since major events can occur outside normal trading hours, positions held overnight are exposed to unpredictable price movements. These moves can cause:
264: - Significant gaps in price when the market opens the next day, potentially resulting in substantial gains or losses.
265: - Limited ability to respond to rapid changes, since liquidity can be low and your ability to modify or exit positions quickly may be constrained.
269: Liquidity tends to be lower during overnight trading sessions because fewer market participants are active. This can lead to:
271: - Wider spreads between the bid and ask prices, increasing transaction costs.
272: - Slippage, where trades execute at a different price than expected due to the lack of market depth.
273: - Difficulty closing large positions without moving the market, potentially causing unfavorable price execution.
279: - Larger-than-expected losses, especially if the price moves against your position.
280: - Stop-loss orders being ineffective, as gaps can skip over your predetermined stop levels, executing your order at a much worse price than expected.
286: - Turn profitable trades into losing ones, especially if held for extended periods.
287: - Vary depending on market conditions, making them harder to predict and plan for.
291: During the night, especially in markets with limited or irregular hours, trade execution may be slower or less precise due to:
293: - Broker operating hours: Some brokers may not support certain trade actions or modifications outside of their regular operating hours, reducing your ability to respond to market conditions.
