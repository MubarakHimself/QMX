## First-order findings

1. **The largest fresh-start conflict is MIS delivery authority.** The wiki says CT-MIS-01 serves Book, KSA, and manifest-bounded bot consumers. Completed Story 3.2 selects an in-process synchronous dispatcher but accepts **Book and KSA only** and explicitly refuses direct bot consumers. This must be decided; it cannot be merged editorially.
2. **SQS was the clearest BMAD-only semantic addition—and is now the clearest semantic-drift example.** The wiki mis-expanded SQS and named the hard-block behavior but contained no formula. Story 3.1 then ratified `sqs_weighted_component_floor_v1`, integer basis-point component qualities/weights, exact input evidence, an unreachable marker, and a minimum reachable score. The 2026-08-17 operator correction establishes **Spread Quality Sensor**; therefore the BMAD aggregate is not recoverable as SQS. Its generic snapshot-health idea is `REOPEN` under a different name, if wanted at all.
3. **Several wiki “open” items were narrowed or concretized after the wiki pass:** cross-node transport, backend PostgreSQL migration discipline, CT-SYNC envelope/behavior proof, acquisition/cleaning/licensing policies, connection recovery, minimal Book/BMS/Treasury mechanics, paper evidence, and the four-part reconciliation decomposition.
4. **A completed story is not always a closed contract.** Story 1.9 is done while deliberately preserving AD-41 Q3 as `PROPOSED, pending operator countersign` and leaving cadence, retention, hash, serialization, auth, venue, and O-6 values unresolved. Likewise, Story 5.8 creates a local CT-PAPER-01 standard while explicitly declining global field ratification.
5. **The execution boundary was never completed.** Stories 4.1–4.3 are backlog, so there is no command adapter, no `amend_order`, and no startup gate wiring. The completed 4.4–4.7 work is supporting semantics around a missing core.
