## 5. Resolve platform-wide composition and extensibility

Preserve these distinctions without making them a compulsory rigid hierarchy:

- A capability is an operation with a declared meaning.
- An extension contributes capabilities and optionally procedures, skills or presentation.
- A workflow composes operations, agents and decisions.
- A mini-app supplies a usable interface around useful capabilities/workflows.
- A widget is a view/control within an interface.
- A complete trading-system composition may also have a headless deployment and an optional mini-app for inspection and operation.

The exploratory surface is the **Experimentation Board**, within the QMX Workflows direction. It may be messy, temporary or partially specified. Not every note, dictionary entry, internal function or variable becomes a node. Conversely, do not force everything into opaque notebooks: selected code, subflows or stages can be exposed where inspection, testing or reuse benefits. The operator can choose useful granularity.

Ship defaults and templates alongside custom construction. The author can work manually, through the copilot, using code/notebooks/CLIs, or in a mixture. Graph editing is not the only programming route.

Define public contribution/discovery and operation contracts: stable identifiers, version compatibility, input/output schemas, cardinality and mapping, configuration/defaults, dependencies, effect class, permissions, resource needs, execution mode, error/refusal shapes, progress, outputs, documentation and validation. Model single items, collections and streams honestly. Explain how one-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-one and many-to-many mappings work. Distinguish zip, keyed join, broadcast and Cartesian expansion; do not silently guess. Validate graph endpoints and full cycles according to the chosen loop semantics.

Resolve drafts, saved runnable versions, executions, attempts, outputs, installed instances, deployments and layouts. Decide what a reusable template captures and what becomes invocation configuration. Distinguish a retry of an uncertain external action from a new deliberate experiment; a run result from a saved projection; a published package from activation on an account. Layout edits alone should not change computation, while explicit rewiring of semantic ports may intentionally change it.

Investigate shared infrastructure rather than automatically duplicating QMA scheduling, stores or registries. Do not force every deterministic calculation through a Quant/Mission solely because QMA already has task graphs. Do not equate a loop with a skill: inspect the current meanings and explain how reusable loop procedures can be packaged without conflating their execution state.

Evaluate extending existing owners, factoring narrowly shared contracts, or adding a focused library. Explain each new package, process, schema, registry and store separately. A broad new framework/data library is not the default; neither is “no new package ever” a substitute for honest ownership analysis. Avoid an untyped universal `execute(anything)` escape hatch masquerading as extensibility.

Design install, configure, enable/disable, validate, upgrade, pin, rollback, dependency removal and export/import for another installation. Some contributions are headless; some have rich UI. No marketplace is required. Keep private authoring lineage and secrets out of distributed packages unless explicitly and legitimately exported. Assess first-party/custom trust and future sharing without inventing SaaS tenancy requirements.
